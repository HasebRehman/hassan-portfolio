FAQs
How does working with you actually go?

You share the idea, I send a plan and quote, we build it together with updates along the way. Simple.

How long does a project take?

Logos usually take 3 - 5 days, websites 1 - 2 weeks. Bigger builds get a clear timeline upfront.

What services do you offer?

Branding, social media, art direction, UI design, websites, apps, web apps and AI chatbots. Basically, if it lives on a screen, I can make it.

What if I don’t like the design?

That's what revisions are for. We tweak until it feels right, within the revisions in your plan.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If we've gone through the revisions and you're still not happy with the final result, you get a full refund.
